# FliHub Polish & Fixes

Future backlog for usability, scoring edge cases, and walkthrough improvements. Phase 1H official score corrections are a separate workflow.

## Mobile scorekeeping controls

**Observed.** Typing strokes on a phone makes it easy to enter an extra digit.

**Expected.** Replace free-text stroke entry with large − and + buttons. Start the control at the hole’s configured par (3 in the demo) and update the displayed score and totals immediately when a score is entered or adjusted. Keep an untouched hole visibly Unscored. Provide an explicit way to record par without first adjusting the value. Unscored holes still block submission.

## Mid-round substitutions

**Observed.** The current workflow locks the athlete lineup once scoring starts. A team may need a substitute during a round.

**Expected.** Record the outgoing athlete, incoming athlete, round, and effective hole. For a substitution effective at Hole 3 before play begins on that hole, Holes 1–2 stay with the outgoing athlete and Hole 3 onward goes to the incoming athlete. Count each hole exactly once in the team total and preserve the scoring and substitution history.

**Open decision.** Decide who gets credit and how to handle any recorded strokes if the outgoing athlete has already thrown or entered a score on the effective hole.

## Team standings walkthrough

**Observed.** The existing standings walkthrough uses solo entries.

**Expected.** Keep the solo example and add a short team walkthrough using the verified six-group disc golf fixture. Focus on one group with two teams of two athletes. Show each athlete’s contribution, how those scores combine into team totals, and where the published team totals appear in the signed-out standings.

## Phase 1A walkthrough timing

**Observed.** The earlier narration follows the visible actions. Later, narration continues while the visible workflow stops progressing.

**Expected.** During the final video review, check scene timing and frozen-frame holds. Restore missing actions or shorten the narration and holds so each section matches what viewers see.

## Button and status colors

**Observed.** The current colors could communicate actions and states more consistently.

**Expected.** Use green for Create actions and blue for In progress indicators. Keep readable labels and adequate contrast so color reinforces the meaning.
