# Phase 1D narration — Competition structure

Recording: `npm run record:walkthrough-1d` (disposable stack; fictional data).
Synthesis input is `recordings/narration-1d.json` (TTS spells "FliHub" as
"Fly Hub"; captions keep the correct spelling).

## s1-league-season — League and division-less season (Riley)

Riley, the organizer at Lakeside Golf Collective, creates a league, then a
season inside it with start and end dates. There are no divisions yet —
FliHub simply runs the season division-less until the organizer needs them.

## s2-direct-entry — Direct season entry (Riley)

Teams don't wait for divisions. Riley enters the Blue Heron Flyers directly
into the season, and the entry shows exactly that: no division assigned.

## s3-division-assign — Division created later, entry assigned (Riley)

When the structure firms up, Riley creates an Open division and assigns the
existing entry to it. The same entry now carries the Open badge in place of
"no division assigned".

## s4-events — Season-linked vs independent events (Riley)

Events work both ways. The Harvest Open stands alone, marked Independent,
while Week 1 is linked to the season and wears its season badge — so an
organizer can tell them apart at a glance.
