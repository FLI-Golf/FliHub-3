Recording: `npm run record:walkthrough-1e` (disposable stack; fictional data).
Synthesis input is `recordings/narration-1e.json` (TTS spells "FliHub" as
"Fly Hub"; captions keep the correct spelling).

## s1-season-roster — A team's season roster (Riley)

Back at Lakeside Golf Collective, Riley opens the Summer 2026 season roster.
The Blue Heron Flyers carry their regular athletes, Ava Stone and Sam Finch —
assigned to this season, so participation always stays with the season.

## s2-reserve-pool — The separate reserve pool (Riley)

Reserves live outside the teams. The season's own men's reserve pool holds
Ben Reed — ready to fill in, but excluded from ordinary scheduling until he
is officially called on.

## s3-substitution — Substitution for one season-linked event (Riley)

For the Week 3 event, Ava can't play. Riley records a substitution: the
event, Ava out, Ben in, plus the reason — one append-only record that names
exactly who replaced whom.

## s4-roster-intact — Roster and reserve pool unchanged (Riley)

And nothing else moved. Ava is still on the season roster, and Ben is still
in the reserve pool — the substitution applies to that one event only, so the
season's participation history stays intact.
