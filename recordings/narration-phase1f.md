"Fly Hub" and "1G" as "one-G"; captions keep the correct spelling). The

## s1-course-layout — Nine holes, Red and Blue positions (Riley)

Riley's nine-hole course at Lakeside is laid out hole by hole — each one
named, with Red and Blue basket positions carrying their own distances in
feet. One course, two ways to play it.

## s2-single-round — Single-round event, no intermission (Riley)

Not every event needs two rounds. Club Night plays this course as a single
round at the Red positions — one round on the schedule, and no intermission
in sight.

## s3-two-rounds — Red, intermission, Blue: 18 holes on nine (Riley)

The FLI demonstration event plays the same nine physical holes twice:
Round 1 at Red, an intermission break, then Round 2 at Blue — eighteen holes
of play on a nine-hole course.

## s4-groups — Six staffed groups (Riley)

Six groups are staffed for the event. Group 1 pairs two teams and their four
rostered athletes, with Casey assigned as the scorekeeper — assignments made
now, so Phase 1G scoring can pick them up.
