# Phase 1G narration — Mobile scorekeeping

Recording: `npm run record:walkthrough-1g` (disposable stack; fictional data;
phone-sized viewport). Synthesis input is `recordings/narration-1g.json` (TTS
spells "FliHub" as "Fly Hub" and "1H" as "one-H"; captions keep the correct
spelling). The recording follows the assigned scorekeeper through the same
fictional FLI demo used in the Phase 1F walkthrough; intervening holes are
prefilled with fictional scores off-camera, and a "Later in the round" slate
skips the repetitive entry. Official approval stays in Phase 1H; the future
playoff shootout is not part of the recording.

Status: recorded — approved by Dustin (2026-11-06); published copies live in
`static/walkthroughs/` and play at `/walkthroughs/phase1g`.

## s1-phone-scorecard — Opening the event on a phone (Casey)

Casey, the assigned scorekeeper, opens the FLI demonstration event on a
phone. The scorecard already knows the setup: Round 1 · 9 holes · Red, an
intermission, then Round 2 · 9 holes · Blue — with Group 1's two teams and
their four athletes.

## s2-draft-and-submit — Draft entry, missing-never-zero, Round 1 submits (Casey)

On hole 1 of Round 1, Casey enters strokes — every entry saves as a draft.
One score is still blank, and the card says exactly why that matters: missing
scores block submission and never count as zero. Casey fills it in, and
Round 1 is submitted.

## s3-second-pass — Intermission, then the second pass at Blue (Casey)

After the intermission, Round 2 replays the same nine physical holes at the
Blue positions. The card displays holes 10 through 18 — displayed hole 10 is
physical hole 1 on the second pass — and each round's scores stay separate.

## s4-final-hole — Later in the round: hole 18 and Submitted (Casey)

Later in the round, the card is nearly complete. Casey enters the final
scores on hole 18 and submits Round 2. Submitted scorecards are locked
against silent changes — official approval and published results arrive in
Phase 1H.
